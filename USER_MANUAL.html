Plain English Intro

What does this website actually do?

Think of DIA as your objective strategic co-pilot. Whenever you or your team face a big crossroad, DIA guides you step-by-step to pick the best path forward.

⚖️
Removes Bias & Guesswork

Humans naturally favor whatever idea they thought of first, or whatever the loudest person in the room wants. DIA evaluates every option fairly against clear numbers.

🛡️
Catches Blind Spots Early

Before you spend money or time, DIA flags hidden risks, missing information, and traps like "sunk cost" or over-optimism.

📊
Board-Ready Justification

Instantly downloads an executive-level PDF report that clearly explains why a decision was made, showing all criteria, scores, and trade-offs.

💡
The Layman Rule of Thumb
If your decision costs significant money, takes months of work, or affects your career/company, run it through DIA first. If it's deciding where to order lunch, don't worry about it!
Fast Track

The 60-Second Quick Start

Follow these 5 simple actions to complete your first evaluation right away.

  • 1.
    Login or Click "Demo Mode": On the login screen, enter your credentials or simply click the gold "Demo Mode" button to load pre-filled real-world data instantly!
  • 2.
    Define Your Challenge (Step 1): Type your decision in one sentence (e.g., "Should we hire an in-house developer or hire an agency?") and choose your industry and timeframe.
  • 3.
    Pick Your Choices (Step 2): Review the options generated by AI. You can add your own or delete ones you don't like (minimum 2 options).
  • 4.
    Choose What Matters Most (Step 3): Pick an evaluation style (like "Balanced" or "Growth Focused") or adjust sliders so the weights total 100%.
  • 5.
    Get Your Results (Step 4): View the winner, test what happens if priorities change, confirm with your human judgment, and download your Executive PDF Report!

Step 1: Define Context (Grounding the AI)

Tell DIA what you are trying to solve and the world you are operating in.

Garbage in, garbage out. The more specific you are here, the sharper DIA's evaluation will be.

Decision Title

Keep it brief and active. Example: "Expand to US market in Q3" or "Buy new CRM software".

Time Horizon & Stakes

Are you deciding for 6 months or 5 years? Are stakes Low, Medium, or High? This changes how much risk DIA tolerates.

📄
Secret Power: Document Upload (PDF / TXT)
Have a vendor quote, proposal, or strategy PDF? Drag it directly into the upload box! DIA reads through it automatically and extracts hidden risks, prices, and facts so you don't have to type them all out.
🎯
Decision Readiness Score
When you click "Analyze Decision Framing", DIA grades your question from 0 to 100%. If your question is too vague (e.g. "Make more money"), it will tell you what missing details to add so you get reliable answers.
2

Step 2: Strategic Options (The Paths Available)

Pick between 2 and 6 distinct paths forward.

A decision is only as good as the options you consider. If you only have one option, you don't have a decision—you have an ultimatum!

✨ AI-Generated Options

DIA suggests 3 to 4 realistic strategies based on your industry and context. You can keep them, tweak their wording, or type your own from scratch.

✏️ Adding & Removing

Click "+ Add Option" to include a wild-card idea. Click the red Trash icon to remove an option that isn't feasible. Keep between 2 and 6 options.

💡
Layman Pro Tip: Always Include "Do Nothing"
Always test your ideas against "Maintain status quo / Do nothing for 6 months". Often, doing nothing is safer than a rushed, expensive bet!
3

Step 3: Strategic Evaluation Mode (1-Click Simplicity)

Choose the strategic lens for your decision. DIA applies the optimal weighted criteria in the background.

No need to fiddle with complicated sliders or percentage math! Simply click the mode that best matches your strategic goal, and DIA configures the multi-criteria weights automatically behind the scenes.

⚖️ Balanced Approach

Recommended for most choices. Evenly balances strategic alignment, financial returns, risk mitigation, and operational feasibility.

🚀 Growth Focused

Prioritizes high revenue upside, aggressive market capture, and high-impact strategic differentiation.

🛡️ Risk Averse & Defensive

Focuses on downside risk containment, compliance, capital preservation, and organizational safety.

⚡ Fast Expansion

Prioritizes execution velocity, short time-to-value, and seizing fast-moving market windows.

✨
Zero Math Required
All 4 modes are pre-configured to total exactly 100% in the backend. Click your preferred mode card and hit "Run analysis"!

No Confusion

Layman Jargon Buster

Click any technical term below to see what it means in simple 5th-grade words.

What does "Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)" mean? ▼
Simple Translation

It just means scoring options like a report card! Instead of picking whatever feels cool, you judge each option across multiple tests (like Cost, Speed, and Quality), multiply them by how much each test matters to you, and see which option gets the highest overall GPA.

What is "Time Horizon"? ▼
Simple Translation

How far into the future you are looking. A short horizon (e.g., 3 months) means you need quick results right away. A long horizon (e.g., 3 to 5 years) means you are willing to wait for bigger, durable returns even if the first year is slow.

What does "Decision Readiness Score" mean? ▼
Simple Translation

Think of it as an "Is your question clear?" meter. If your score is 85%, your problem statement has clear goals, realistic constraints, and timeline. If your score is 30%, DIA is telling you: "Hey, this problem is too blurry—tell me more about your budget and goals before we make a choice."

What is "Sunk Cost Bias"? ▼
Simple Translation

The trap of continuing to pour money into a failing project just because you already spent a lot on it in the past. Money spent in the past is gone forever. DIA alerts you if you are picking an option out of stubbornness rather than future value.

What is "Sensitivity / Scenario Simulation"? ▼
Simple Translation

Asking "What if?". For example: "What if our revenue drops next month and cost suddenly becomes twice as important?" By dragging sliders, you can test if your winning option is still the winner when priorities change.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick solutions to common questions when using DIA.

How do I start using DIA without creating an account? ▼

On the login screen, simply click the gold "Demo Mode" button. This logs you into a full executive sandbox with pre-loaded case studies so you can test all features without entering an email or password.

Can I change my answers if I made a mistake in Step 1 or 2? ▼

Yes! You can click the "Back" button at any step, or click the numbers (1, 2, 3) in the top stepper bar to jump back and edit your text anytime.

Why won't Step 3 let me proceed to Step 4? ▼

Check the bottom of Step 3! Your criteria weights must add up to exactly 100%. If your total is 90% or 110%, the button will alert you to adjust one of the sliders up or down until it reaches 100%.

Where do my downloaded reports go? ▼

When you click "Download report", your browser downloads a file named Decision_Report_[Title].pdf into your computer's Downloads folder. You can also print it directly or email it to colleagues.

Is my company's confidential data safe? ▼

Yes. All evaluations are encrypted and stored in your private session database. Your files and decision texts are never shared with public repositories.
